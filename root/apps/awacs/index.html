How does it work?

This is an educational geometry model. Catalogue ranges, RCS values and detection curves are illustrative, not validated aircraft performance.

Terrain from Mapzen / AWS Terrain Tiles is bundled locally in data/terrain/. Global zoom levels 0–5 cover Mercator latitudes ±85.05°. The map renders real elevation at 1× scale. Aircraft altitude is feet above sea level, independent of ground elevation.

Visibility tests the straight chord between two aircraft against a spherical Earth (6,371 km radius), sampling approximately every 2 km. Terrain is decoded from the same local zoom-5 Terrarium tiles used by the map, approximately 2.5–5 km pixel spacing at low to middle latitudes. Narrow ridges may be missed. No atmospheric refraction, clutter, electronic warfare, aspect or weather model is included.

The overlay samples up to 3° bearings and 10 km radial steps, at the reference altitude. Green means line of sight; purple means masked or below terrain; grey means missing elevation. The dashed arc is instrument range, not airframe endurance range. Target results also apply field of view, slant range, RCS and the catalogue function.

LOST = grey dot · DETECT = yellow glow · LOCK = red glow. Heading is clockwise from true north. A 270° field is centred on heading. A 360° field is unaffected by heading.

Data or curve failures are shown as unknown, never treated as confirmed visibility. Changes save in this browser automatically. WebGL and a static HTTP/HTTPS server are required. Flags load from FlagCDN; map, terrain, fonts and scripts are local.
